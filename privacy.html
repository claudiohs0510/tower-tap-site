Tower Tap: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026.

What the app collects

Tower Tap itself does not ask for an account and does not read your contacts, location, photos or microphone. The only data that leaves your phone is what the ad service below collects.

What stays on your phone

Your best score and your sound settings are saved on your phone only. Deleting the app removes them.

Ads

Tower Tap shows full-screen ads after every third game. The ads come from Google AdMob. Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location, and how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads. On iPhone the app first asks your permission to track you across other apps and websites; if you say no, ads still appear but are not personalised. In some regions Google also shows a consent form. See Google's own privacy policy for what Google does with this data. You can reset or limit tracking in iPhone Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking.

Children

Tower Tap is not made for children under 13 and is not in the Kids category.
