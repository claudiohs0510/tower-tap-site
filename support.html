Tower Tap: Support

How to play

Tap anywhere (or press Space) to drop the sliding block. Land it clean to keep it wide and build a streak. A miss cuts the block down. When nothing is left, the run ends.

Common questions

  • No sound? Check the Sound button in the game and the silent switch on your iPhone.
  • Lost my best score? It is saved on the phone only; deleting the app removes it.
  • Something broke? Email claudiohs.0510@gmail.com with your iPhone model and iOS version.
